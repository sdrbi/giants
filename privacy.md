# Giants Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 25, 2026*

Giants is an app for exploring the papers that cite a scholarly work. It is designed to collect nothing about you.

**What stays on your device.** The trees you plant, their papers, your settings, and any OpenAlex API key you enter (kept in your device's Keychain) are stored only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, and we cannot see any of it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

**What is sent, and to whom.** To build and update trees, Giants sends requests directly from your device to OpenAlex (api.openalex.org), a free, open index of scholarly works run by the nonprofit OurResearch. These requests contain DOIs and OpenAlex paper IDs, plus your API key if you entered one. OpenAlex sees your device's IP address, as any website does. OpenAlex's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

**No tracking or analytics.** Giants contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and does not share data with anyone.

**Notifications.** If you allow them, Giants shows notifications when your trees gain new citations. These are created on your device.

**Purchases.** Optional tips are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. We receive no personal or payment information.

**Contact.** Questions about privacy: open an issue at [github.com/sdrbi/giants/issues](https://github.com/sdrbi/giants/issues).
