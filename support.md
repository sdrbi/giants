# Giants Support

**Found a bug, have a question, or want a feature?** [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/singingbing-code/giants/issues/new). Include your device (iPhone, iPad, or Mac), and the DOI of the tree if the problem is with a particular one.

## Common questions

**A tree stopped growing and says it's waiting for the daily limit.**
Giants uses OpenAlex, which gives each device a free daily allowance of about 1,000 searches. Very large trees can use it up. Giants pauses and continues by itself after the allowance resets. For ten times the allowance, get a free API key at [openalex.org](https://openalex.org/settings/api) and paste it into Settings ▸ OpenAlex.

**Why does a paper have fewer citing papers in Giants than on Google Scholar?**
Giants only shows citations that OpenAlex has indexed, and leaves out errata, editorials, and similar non-research items. Coverage varies by field and publisher.

**What are the rhombus-shaped papers?**
Papers the tree didn't explore further because it reached its paper limit. Tap one and choose Grow Further.

**How do I make my trees take less space?**
Settings ▸ Abstracts shows how much space abstracts use and can remove them.

**Where is my data?**
Only on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).
